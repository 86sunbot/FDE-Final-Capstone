# Participant Challenge Brief

You have inherited a multinational Cell & Gene Therapy orchestration estate. Senior leadership wants the next product generation to scale globally while reducing patient-at-risk cases, manual coordination and avoidable delays.

Your job is to **discover before designing**.

## Deliverables
1. Brownfield forensic assessment with evidence.
2. Domain model and current-state journey/state reconstruction.
3. Prioritized problem statement and measurable KPIs.
4. Requirements (functional + NFR + assurance).
5. Target architecture with ADRs and migration strategy.
6. Three workflow POCs demonstrating engineering value.
7. Evaluation/TEVV strategy including edge, adversarial, outage and authority cases.
8. Security, privacy, responsible-AI and governance analysis.
9. Resilience/recovery design and operational handover.
10. Production-readiness gap assessment and 90-day roadmap.

Do not assume every problem requires AI. Show where deterministic engineering is superior.
