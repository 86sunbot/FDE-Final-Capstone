# Stage 19 - Executive Decision Story

The brownfield problem was not “missing AI.” It was fragmented evidence, ambiguous identity, conflicting system authority, unsafe shortcuts, weak retry semantics and unowned exceptions. The capstone replaced those semantics with a deterministic evidence and decision layer, then placed AI outside the authority boundary.

The three POCs show one coherent control model from patient readiness through slot orchestration to Quality release. The important result is not fluent text; it is that unknown stays unknown, conflicts remain visible, decisions cite evidence, retries do not duplicate effects and only authenticated humans can make consequential decisions.

The assistant is deliberately optional. Its architecture is safe enough for controlled future evaluation, but its value has not been proven. The next business decision is not “deploy”; it is whether to fund real stakeholder validation, controlled policies, integration simulators, human factors and independent assurance.
