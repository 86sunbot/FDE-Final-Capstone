# Stage 16 - Training, SOP and Evidence Index

## Academic operator briefing

- Understand synthetic-only scope and evidence/authority separation.
- Read deterministic facts before recommendations.
- Inspect material evidence IDs and uncertainty.
- Never treat AI, MES or ERP as Quality release authority.
- Do not retry unknown external effects.
- Use reject/abstain/escalate and maintain case ownership.
- Preserve the original ZIP and generated evidence.
