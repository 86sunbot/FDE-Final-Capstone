## Claim boundary

The **source-case explorer** reads original v2 synthetic rows and their digests. The **three-POC run** uses a separate scripted fixture to prove control behavior. The **inject preview** derives a zero-slack *assumption* and never writes a schedule. The frozen catalog report marks which expected properties were asserted and which were not. A presenter must not call the explorer a migrated data platform, the preview an optimizer, or the 55 structural passes full validation.
